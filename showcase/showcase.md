# Facet

Seven kinds of cut stone. One good swap. A chain waiting to happen. Take hold of a gem, carry it onto its neighbor, and let go to make a line of three or more. Watch the stones shatter and the board fall into a new possibility.

The stones you leave behind remember the pressure. Each nearby clear deepens their cracks until they become flawed — ready to join any clear that touches them, and worth twice the points. Work a worn corner, then find the move that releases it all.

Longer lines earn exceptional cuts. A brilliant takes the ring around it. A star cuts through its whole row and column. A prism trades its place for every stone of one kind. Combine them with a stretch of flawed gems and watch the reaction travel across the field, each step worth more than the last.

Reach the target to open the next level with a fresh board and a higher ambition. There is no clock pushing you: study the facets, make your offer, and pull it back if you change your mind. The round ends only when no productive move remains.
